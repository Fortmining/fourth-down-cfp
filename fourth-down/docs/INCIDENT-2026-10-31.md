## Root cause versus remaining uncertainty

The halftime failure point is confirmed: **the worker locally rejected an alert the server had selected**. The older receipt records only broad flags; it does not retain matching-follow identity/type/timing facts. The October 31 inspection shows correct choices now and cannot reconstruct IndexedDB at 5:16 PM. Read-only offline evaluation of the exact production selected() predicate with the newly inspected follow snapshot selects halftime correctly; it is not historical delivery proof.

No evidence establishes why that worker rejected halftime, or whether later pushes failed to reach the worker, were delayed/expired, or could not report receipts. Current notification permission does not establish an iOS banner was shown. Provider success responses previously discarded the numeric HTTP status, so old accepted records prove successful completion of sendNotification, not a retained exact status code.

The owner also has provider-accepted Minnesota–Indiana alerts without display receipts. Another browser's Ohio State–USC follow has accepted events without receipts. This rules out a universal event-generation/provider-rejection outage, but does NOT prove other browsers displayed alerts or establish that every followed game/device is affected.
